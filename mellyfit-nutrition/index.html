Meet Melissa

Melissa Cosentino is a Certified Nutritional Practitioner with a diploma from the Institute of Holistic Nutrition, and the founder of MellyFit Nutrition. Her mission is to help people carve out their own unique path to a fit body, mind and soul.

She specializes in digestive health, stress management and detoxification, and has helped hundreds of people with food allergies and sensitivities, fitness nutrition, postpartum wellness, meal planning and weight loss. She runs signature group programs like the 7-Day Holistic Cleanse and a Digestive Health Program, built on functional testing and a root-cause approach.

She understands how easily health slips to the back burner in a busy life, and her goal is to give you the education, tools and support to take control of it for good.
