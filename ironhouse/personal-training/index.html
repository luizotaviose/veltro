Personal training

One coach. One plan. You.

Private coaching for people who want every session to count. It starts with a free assessment — no obligation, no sales pitch.

How personal training works

  1. 01

    Assess

    Movement screen, training history, injuries, schedule and goals — before you lift anything.

  2. 02

    Program

    A written 4–12 week block built around your body and calendar, adjusted every session.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Lifts, body composition and conditioning tests tracked so progress is never a guess.

  4. 04

    Support

    Simple nutrition, sleep and recovery habits that make the training work.

FAQ.

Who is personal training for?

Anyone who wants the most precise, fastest progress: beginners who want confidence, athletes preparing for a season, and people returning from injury after physio clearance.

What happens at the free assessment?

45 minutes with a coach: a conversation about goals and history, a movement screen, and a short sample session. You leave with a recommendation, whether or not you sign up.

Can I share sessions with a partner?

Yes. Semi-private sessions for two people are $130 per session, split however you like.

What's your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel up to 12 hours before your session at no cost. Later cancellations use the session.
