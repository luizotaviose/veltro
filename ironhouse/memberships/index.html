Fair terms

No fine print.

Cancel any time

Monthly plans end with 7 days' notice, online or at the front desk. No cancellation fee, no certified letters.

Annual plans

A 12-month term paid up front. Full refund within the first 14 days; after that, transferable to a friend.

Freeze when life happens

Up to 8 weeks a year for travel, injury or parental leave, free of charge.

Enrollment fee

$49 one-time on monthly Essential and Performance. Waived on annual plans and on Elite.

Price lock

Your rate stays the same for as long as your membership stays active.

Membership FAQ.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans are month to month — cancel any time with 7 days' notice. Annual plans are a 12-month term paid up front, with a full refund if you cancel within the first 14 days.

What does the enrollment fee cover?

Your onboarding assessment, app setup and a locker key. It is waived on all annual plans and on Elite.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Freeze for up to 8 weeks a year for travel, injury or parental leave at no charge.

Can I switch plans later?

Upgrade any time and the difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect from your next billing date.

Are taxes included?

No. All prices are in Canadian dollars and HST (13%) is added at checkout.

Do you offer discounts?

Students, first responders and healthcare workers receive 15% off any monthly plan with valid ID.
