Good to know.

I've never lifted. Is IRONHOUSE for me?

Yes. Around half our members started as complete beginners. Every lift is taught from scratch and scaled to you, even inside a group class.

What happens on a free tour?

You meet a coach, see the floor, talk through your goals and — if you want — train in a real class. No sales pitch in a back office.

How big are the classes?

Strength and Athlete Lab are capped at six people per coach. Conditioning and mobility sessions run up to twelve to fourteen.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes, flat shoes and a water bottle. Towels, chalk, lockers and showers are on us.
