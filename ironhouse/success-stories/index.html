Success stories

Real members. Real work.

No before-and-after photos and no miracle claims — just members describing what consistent, coached training changed for them.

01

+90 lb

Deadlift in six months

PS

Priya S.

Performance · member since 2024

“I started at 135 and couldn't keep my back straight. At testing day I pulled 225 — with the whole class cheering.”

I'd done classes at big-box gyms for years and never really got stronger. The first thing Marcus did was slow everything down and teach me to brace.

Six months later I hit a number I never thought I'd see. What surprised me more was how much better my back feels sitting at a desk all day.

Tom W.

Elite → Performance · member since 2023

“No crash diet. Three sessions a week and Dana's habit checklist. My doctor asked what I'd changed.”

I started with private sessions because I was nervous about keeping up. After four months I moved into small groups and haven't missed a week since.

The structure is what works for me. I know exactly what I'm doing and why.

MC

Maria C.

Performance · member since 2024

“I'd never been in a gym in my life. Lena had me doing my first real pull-up before my 53rd birthday.”
