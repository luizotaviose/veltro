Our story

A neighbourhood chair since 2014.

Crown & Blade started with one chair and one simple idea: take the time to get it right. No rushing, no assembly line, just a good conversation and a cut you'll get compliments on.

Today the shop is a barbershop and salon in one, with a small team that remembers your name and how you like it. Come in for a fade, stay for a coffee.
