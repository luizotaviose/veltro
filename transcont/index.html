06 / FAQ

Questions we hear first.

Still unsure?

Ask a person, not a ticket queue.

Write your question in the form. The answer comes from the same person who will handle your deal.

Ask a question
How do you find buyers and suppliers?

Through our contacts, trade networks and direct search in the markets that fit your product. Every lead is checked before we present it to you.

Who do you work for, the buyer or the seller?

For the side that hires us. We make that clear to the other side from the first contact, and we never hide terms from you.

How do you charge?

Our fee is agreed in writing before we start, and it depends on the type and size of the deal. Tell us about yours and we explain it.

Do you handle the shipping too?

Yes. After the contract is signed, we coordinate the container with partner carriers and follow it until delivery.

What products can you trade?

Goods that move in containers, from food and raw materials to manufactured products. Some goods need permits, and we check that before any offer.
