Meet Terence

Terence Boateng earned a BSc in Honours Kinesiology from the University of Waterloo, then a BASc and MHSc in Nutrition from Ryerson University. That mix of exercise and nutrition science lets him work exactly where the two meet.

He specializes in weight loss, muscle gain, metabolic health and digestive health, and regularly works with professional and amateur athletes, especially combat sports athletes. He trains and competes in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu himself, using emerging nutrition research to fuel his own goals.
