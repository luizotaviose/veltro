FAQ

Questions we hear every week.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We prepare drawings, apply for City of Toronto or municipal permits when they're required, and book every inspection. Permit fees are listed separately in your estimate.

How accurate is the estimate?

It's a fixed, line-by-line price for the scope we agreed on. The only changes are ones you ask for, or hidden conditions we show you with photos and a written change order first.

Can we stay in the house during the renovation?

Almost always. We put up dust barriers, protect floors and clean up every evening. For full-kitchen jobs we can set up a temporary kitchenette.

How does payment work?

A deposit to book your start date, progress payments tied to clear milestones, and a final payment only after the walkthrough list is complete.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years on all workmanship, plus the manufacturer warranties on materials and appliances. If something we built fails, we come back and fix it.
