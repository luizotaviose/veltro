Celiac disease (gluten intolerance)

Our Registered Dietitian Nutritionists can help those with Celiac Disease or a Gluten Intolerance maintain proper health on a gluten free diet. Whether you’re newly diagnosed or have been gluten free for life, our Registered Dietitian Nutritionists will develop a tailored celiac diet plan full of delicious and nutritious gluten free foods and gluten free snacks to help you improve your health.

Good to know before you book

Covered by most insurance

Sessions are covered when your extended health benefits include a Registered Dietitian. You get an insurance invoice for every session you attend.

Anywhere in Ontario

Sessions by webcam, telephone or email for Ontario residents. Some in-person sessions at Yonge and Eglinton, depending on RD availability.

Physician first

RD care is secondary to your family physician. Recent weight, height and bloodwork should come from your doctor.
