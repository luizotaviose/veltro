    How care starts, and what it costs

    Every new patient begins with a full assessment. Your dietitian then recommends a treatment plan bundle. Insurance invoices are issued for each session you attend.

    1Nutrition assessment

    Full nutrition assessment

    $120+ HST

    • One session with a licensed RD, up to 60 minutes, by webcam, phone or email
    • Diet analysis and check-up
    • Goal recommendations before a treatment plan starts
    • Foundational resources and a grocery tour guide by email

    Required for all new patients. Paid at the end of the session, with an insurance invoice.

    For adults, 16 and over

    Long-term treatment plan

    $300+ HST

    • 3 RD sessions, up to 40 minutes each
    • 4 personalized meal plans, usable for 2 to 3 months
    • 12 meal ideas or recipes and 4 to 6 snack options
    • Guides on mindful eating, intuitive eating, sports nutrition and sleep
    • Best for 2 to 3 nutrition goals

    Three insurance invoices of $100 + HST, one after each session.

    Covered by most extended health benefits plans

    Sessions are covered when your policy includes a Registered Dietitian. Check with your insurance provider to confirm your coverage before you book.

    • An insurance invoice for every sessionBundles are paid when they start, and invoices are issued after each session you attend.
    • For Ontario residentsSessions by webcam, phone or email anywhere in the province. Some in-person sessions depending on RD availability.
    • Physician firstRD care is secondary to your family physician. Recent weight, height and bloodwork come from your doctor.
