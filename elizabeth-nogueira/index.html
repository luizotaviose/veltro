Hi, I'm Elizabeth

I know what it's like to feel inflamed, tired, weighed down and disconnected from your body. What began as a wish to make myself holistically healthy, through exercise and proper nutrition, became a passion for helping others see themselves in a new light.

I studied at the Canadian School of Natural Nutrition, where I learned how foods, supplements and natural products can help manage illness and optimize health. Today I combine holistic nutrition with lymphatic drainage, infrared sauna blanket therapy and Reiki, so every session supports the whole you, not just the symptoms.
